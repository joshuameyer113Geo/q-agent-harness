## Canon rules (non-negotiable for all writers)

- Real people get only what's on their public record. Anything sinister goes to fictional characters and fictional programs. Real agencies and hearings can be background.
- Veilwar's four races: Humans, Gray Aliens, Reptilians, Intergalactic Royal Nordics. Mantids are planned, not yet active.
- Reptilian canon is a terrestrial dinosaur line with a Hollow Earth home. No "secret elite" bloodline. This avoids the antisemitic conspiracy form of the trope. Keep it that way.
- Nazi-occult "Aryan" overlays on Nordic lore get reframed as the Nordics' own propaganda, exposed and defeated in-game.
- The game's Ice-Shelf Citadel belongs to the Nordics and draws on the New Swabia myth as in-world legend. It does not present a Nazi base as historical.
- Do not use Maj. Gen. William McCasland's 2026 disappearance as a plot device. It's a real, ongoing missing-person case with no evidence tying it to UAP.
- Zeta Reticuli is where the Grays told humans they came from. In Veilwar canon it's a navigational waypoint or cover address for future humans. This reconciles the Lazar briefing with the canon that Grays are future humans.
- Element 115 is already in the game as the `e115` supply. It's real (moscovium, synthesized 2003, named 2016), unstable, decays in milliseconds, made a few atoms at a time. Never enough of it. That instability is the design.
- The in-game "treaty" can be real in fiction. The authentic touch is that the real world's Greada Treaty is a documented disinformation product from the Bennewitz operation.

## Source taxonomy

- **[DOC]**: primary source, official record, or established fact. Citeable.
- **[CLAIM]**: a witness's or proponent's assertion. Usable as character belief, not as world fact.
- **[MYTH]**: folklore or later invention. Usable as in-world legend the game can expose as a lie.

## Locked-in story beats by era

### WWII prelude (1942-1945)
- Battle of Los Angeles, Feb 25 1942 [DOC]: radar contact over Santa Monica Bay, ~1,400-1,500 antiaaircraft rounds fired. Japan denied overflight. Official records inconclusive.
- Foo fighters [DOC reported, cause unknown]: 415th Night Fighter Squadron, Beaufighters, Rhine Valley, Nov 1944. Eight to ten bright orange lights pacing them, invisible to radar. Donald Meiers borrowed "foo" from Smokey Stover. AP broke it Jan 1 1945.
- Nazi saucers: Haunebu, Vril, Die Glocke are [MYTH]. No pre-1946 documentary evidence. Feuerball and Kugelblitz trace to Vesco's 1968 book. Vril and Haunebu surface ~40 years after the war. Die Glocke dates to 2000 (Witkowski). The hook: the Nordics tried to hand the regime something, it didn't work, and the wonder-weapon legend is postwar fantasy the game shows as a lie.
- Operation Paperclip [DOC]: US recruited German scientists including former Nazi Party members. A real moral stain the dark-state arc references without inventing wrongdoing by named people.

### Roswell 1947
- Jul 8 1947: RAAF announced recovery of a "flying disc." Maj. Jesse Marcel collected debris from Foster ranch. Next day, Eighth Air Force re-identified it as a radar-target weather balloon [DOC].
- 1994 USAF report: debris most likely Project Mogul, classified high-altitude balloon array [DOC]. 1997 report attributed alien-body stories to 1950s anthropomorphic dummy drops [DOC].
- 1995 GAO search: RAAF outgoing administrative messages 1946-49 destroyed. Only unit history and an FBI teletype survived [DOC].
- Later witness claims of bodies and memory metal are [CLAIM]; AARO Vol. 1 (2024) found no evidence of extraterrestrial origin [DOC].
- Story hook: the first cover story is literally a balloon. That's the template for the deception beat.

### Cold War wave (1947-1969)
- Kenneth Arnold, Jun 24 1947, near Mt. Rainier: gave us "flying saucers" [DOC].
- Project Sign (1948), Grudge (1949), Blue Book (1952-1969): 12,618 reports, 701 unidentified. Capt. Edward Ruppelt coined "UFO" [DOC].
- 1953 Robertson Panel recommended debunking [DOC]. 1968 Condon Report closed Blue Book [DOC].
- Sign's 1948 "Estimate of the Situation" favoring an interplanetary explanation was described by Ruppelt but no copy has surfaced [CLAIM/contested].
- 1952 Washington DC radar flap: radar returns over the capital, largest Pentagon press conference since WWII [DOC].
- Paul Bennewitz: AFOSI fed him fabricated alien-base and treaty material in the 1980s. Agent Richard Doty and researcher William Moore later acknowledged it [DOC]. Dulce Base, the Dulce War, and the Greada Treaty grew out of that operation plus John Lear and Bill Cooper [MYTH], tracing to Gerald Light's 1954 letter speculating Eisenhower visited Muroc during his Palm Springs dental-emergency trip [MYTH].

### Betty and Barney Hill, Sept 19-20 1961
- Route 3 through the White Mountains: Franconia Notch, Cannon Mountain, The Flume, Indian Head. A light that seemed to follow them. Figures behind a window through binoculars. Beeping. Two hours late to Portsmouth: missing time [DOC: their account; event unverified].
- Mixed-race couple, both NAACP civil-rights activists. Real stress and scrutiny [DOC].
- Dr. Benjamin Simon hypnotized them weekly in 1964. Simon leaned toward Betty's dreams shaping both accounts; he did not conclude literal abduction [DOC].
- Barney's Feb 1964 sketch includes the wraparound eyes that became the Gray archetype. Skeptics note The Outer Limits episode "The Bellero Shield" aired 12 days earlier [DOC].
- Marjorie Fish matched the star map to Zeta Reticuli (1968-69, Gliese catalog). Later Gaia DR3 analyses show the match isn't unique: alternatives include Epsilon Eridani and Epsilon Indi [DOC: analysis; CLAIM: interpretation].

### Bob Lazar, S-4, element 115 (1989)
- May 1989: spoke on KLAS-TV as "Dennis," face hidden. Went public under his name that November. Claimed EG&G interview, Navy employment, work at S-4 near Papoose Lake south of Groom Lake [DOC].
- Claimed nine craft. The Sport Model: a disc, skin like liquid titanium, three gravity amplifiers underneath, reactor fueled by stable element 115. Occupant seats child-sized. Briefing docs described alien involvement over 10,000 years. Codnames Galileo, Sidekick, Looking Glass appear in no record [CLAIM]. Note: existing Drive prologue says 52 ft; Lazar often gives about 40 ft. Pick one.
- Element 115 (moscovium) synthesized 2003, named 2016. Every isotope decays in milliseconds. No bench-stable fuel exists [DOC].
- MIT and Caltech have no record of him. Worked at Los Alamos as a contractor technician; 1982 lab phonebook lists him. Pleaded guilty to pandering in 1990 [DOC].
- If the game needs an S-4 insider, use a fictional engineer at a fictional S-4 annex.

### Phoenix Lights, Mar 13 1997
- Two separate events on the best viewing night for Comet Hale-Bopp.
- ~8:00 p.m.: V or boomerang of lights moving SE across Nevada and Arizona, explained as five A-10s from Operation Snowbird in formation lights [DOC].
- ~10:00 p.m.: row of hovering lights over the Sierra Estrella, explained as LUU-2B/B parachute illumination flares dropped by Maryland ANG A-10s over the Barry M. Goldwater Range. Flares hover because heat inflates chutes, then wink out behind the ridge [DOC].
- Gov. Fife Symington mocked it in June 1997 with an aide in an alien costume; in 2007 he said he saw something otherworldly [DOC].
- Some witnesses insist the 8 p.m. object was one solid craft a mile wide. The only video shows lights moving relative to each other [CLAIM].
- This is the deception beat's best real-world anchor: two truths overlap, giving officials a perfect mundane explanation.

### Nimitz Tic Tac, Nov 10-14 2004
- Nimitz Carrier Strike Group on COMPTUEX about 100 nmi SW of San Diego, off Baja. USS Princeton's AN/SPY-1B radar tracked anomalous aerial vehicles for days: above 80,000 ft, about 28,000 ft at about 100 kt, dropping to the surface in about 0.78 seconds from 28,000 ft [DOC: witness and Navy record].
- Nov 14: Cmdr. David Fravor and Lt. Cmdr. Alex Dietrich saw a churning patch of white water and a white, about 40 ft, wingless, featureless Tic Tac about 50 ft above it. It mirrored Fravor's descending circle, then accelerated away. Princeton reported it about 60 nmi away at the CAP point within about a minute [DOC: testimony; Fravor testified under oath Jul 26 2023].
- Lt. Chad Underwood recorded it on the ATFLIR pod: FLIR1, showing apparent radar jamming or lock-break behavior [DOC]. DoD released FLIR1, GIMBAL, GOFAST in Apr 2020.
- Sensor behaviors to reuse: returns falling like snow from above the scan volume; hover-to-hypersonic with no plume; APG-73 unable to lock; IR showing no hot exhaust; surface disturbance with no visible submerged object (a transmedium hint).

### Disclosure era (2017-today)
- AAWSAP/AATIP funded through DIA, about $22M (2008-2012) [DOC]. Dec 16 2017 NYT story: "Glowing Auras and Black Money." UAP Task Force formed 2020. ODNI Preliminary Assessment Jun 2021: 144 reports, 1 explained, as a balloon [DOC]. AARO established 2022 [DOC].
- AARO resolves most cases as balloons, birds, drones, and clutter, with a residue that stays unresolved. Authentic texture for the balloons-and-birds deception beat: the public is shown real resolved cases while the hard residue is quietly compartmented [DOC].
- AARO Historical Record Report Vol. 1 (2024): no empirical evidence the USG or industry reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology. Named hidden programs either don't exist, are misidentified authentic sensitive programs, or were a disestablished effort. KONA BLUE was a proposed DHS Prospective SAP for non-human biologics, never approved or funded. One IC Controlled Access Program expanded in 2021 to include UAP reverse-engineering, recovered nothing, disestablished. Narrative called largely circular reporting [DOC].
- Art's Parts metal (magnesium, zinc, bismuth layers) analyzed by Oak Ridge for AARO 2024: terrestrial isotopes, likely a manufactured test or byproduct [DOC].
- David Grusch, Jul 26 2023 hearing: former NRO and NGA intelligence officer, UAPTF/AARO representative. Filed a PPD-19 Urgent Concern with the ICIG, which he says was found credible and urgent. Testified he was informed of a multi-decade UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering program and was denied access. Said non-human biologics were recovered per people with direct knowledge. Hadn't personally seen craft or bodies; based it on about 40 witnesses [DOC: testimony]. DoD said no verifiable information supporting such programs [DOC]. Ryan Graves described a dark gray or black cube inside a clear sphere seen by his squadron off Virginia Beach 2014-15.
- UAP Disclosure Act (Schumer-Rounds, 2023 NDAA): defined non-human intelligence, technologies of unknown origin, and legacy program. Proposed a review board and eminent domain over recovered material. Largely stripped in conference [DOC].
- Nov 13 2024 hearing included an alleged Immaculate Constellation program entered into the record. Pentagon denied it exists [DOC/CLAIM].
- 2026 record: White House directed a release program in Feb 2026. Department of War's PURSUE posted six tranches between May 8 and Sep 18 2026, all unresolved cases, rolling basis [DOC].

## Alien archetypes

- Grays [DOC cultural history]: 3.5-4.5 ft, large heads, black wraparound eyes, slit mouth. Template is Barney Hill 1964, cemented by Strieber's Communion 1987. Abduction researchers describe short worker Grays and taller leader beings, examination tables, implants, hybrid programs, screen memories. Thomas Bullard's canonical sequence: capture, exam, conference, tour, otherworldly journey, theophany, return, aftermath. Zeta Reticuli is the folk home.
- Nordics [DOC cultural history]: tall, blond, fair, telepathic, often benevolent and warning about nuclear war. 1950s contactee wave: George Adamski's Orthon from Venus (Desert Center CA, Nov 1952), Giant Rock conventions (George Van Tassel), Billy Meier's Pleiadians (Semjase).
- Reptilians [DOC cultural history]: reptilian humanoids in some abduction reports. Draco/Alpha Draconis shape-shifting elite version popularized by David Icke from the 1990s, widely criticized as antisemitic in conspiracy form. Veilwar canon avoids that trope. Naga and Quetzalcoatl serpent myths are separate world folklore, usable as in-world surfacing legends. The Silurian Hypothesis (Schmidt and Frank, 2018) is a real paper asking whether we'd detect a pre-human industrial civilization in the geologic record.
- Mantids [DOC cultural history]: 6-9 ft, insectoid, praying-mantis overseers who direct Grays, telepathic, calm and clinical. Often reported showing environmental or apocalyptic visions. Appear in Strieber-era and 1990s abduction reports and in DMT-entity reports, which are hallucinatory and not evidence of abduction.

## Underwater UFOs and transmedium (added Oct 15 2026)

- USOs: decades of witness reports of objects entering or leaving the sea. Anecdotal collections, not verified structures [CLAIM].
- Santa Catalina / San Pedro Channel and Malibu: Preston Dennett catalogues reports arguing for an undersea base. No physical evidence [CLAIM].
- Puerto Rico: Laguna Cartagena is a long-running local USO hotspot in witness lore [CLAIM]. The 2013 Aguadilla infrared video, often cited as an object entering the water, was assessed by AARO in 2024 as two objects moving at wind speed that did not enter the water [DOC].
- Nimitz water disturbance [DOC that it was reported; cause unknown]: Fravor testified the four aircrew saw white water on a calm sea with a Tic Tac above it. On return, the disturbance was gone. Dietrich described an ocean churning.
- Transmedium in law: FY2023 NDAA (Pub. L. 117-263) defines transmedium objects or devices as objects observed transitioning between space and atmosphere or between atmosphere and water, including submerged objects in the UAP definition. In force for the Jul 26 2023 House Oversight hearing.
- New Swabia: the German Antarctic Expedition 1938-39 really did fly over and map part of Dronning Maud Land and name it Neuschwabenland [DOC]. The secret Nazi base Base 211, U-boat escapes, and the claim that Operation Highjump was a battle are myth; a peer-reviewed study shows no base was built [MYTH].

## Themes

1. Every era's cover story is mundane (balloon, flare, bird, drone, clutter), and the mundane explanation is often true for some cases. That's what makes it work.
2. The paper trail is the battlefield: destroyed RAAF messages, the missing Estimate, PPD-19 filings, PURSUE tranches.
3. Messenger vs. message: credibility attacks (Lazar's real record problems) and documented disinformation (Bennewitz) both muddy the water.
4. Sensors beat stories: SPY-1, ATFLIR, and radar tapes are the strongest evidence, which sets up the Humans' AI advantage.
5. Oversight vs. compartments: Congress keeps asking; compartments keep saying no evidence. A fictional dark state can live in that gap.
